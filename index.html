I am currently a Visiting Researcher at WAVLab, within the Language Technologies Institute (LTI) at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), hosted by Prof. Shinji Watanabe. I joined CMU in May 2026.

I am also a final-year PhD candidate in the Institute of Computer Science and Engineering at NYCU, advised by Prof. Wen-Chih Peng and Prof. Ping-Chun Hsieh. I am affiliated with the Advanced Database System Laboratory (ADSL) and the RL & Bandits Lab (REAL).

My research focuses on (1) test-time optimization for LLMs, (2) reinforcement learning, and (3) agentic AI applications. I am especially interested in using feedback and sequential decision-making to improve model behavior at inference time. I also maintain a strong interest in the cross-disciplinary applications of sport data science to badminton. Outside research, I find joy in playing tennis.

I am actively seeking Research Scientist and Applied Scientist positions in test-time optimization, reinforcement learning, and agentic AI. I expect to complete my PhD in September 2027. Contact me about opportunities.
